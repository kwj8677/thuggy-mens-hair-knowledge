# Specialty and Textured-Hair Glossary

[한국어 원문](../specialty-hair-glossary.md)

- **Braids** — interwoven strands; discuss length, section size, tension and wear time.
- **Cornrows** — braids formed close to the scalp; discuss parting design, scalp sensitivity and tension.
- **Locs / dreadlocks** — intentionally formed and maintained loc structures; discuss starting method, maturity, maintenance and retwisting.
- **Afro / textured-hair work** — cutting and shaping that respects curl/coily texture, shrinkage and volume.
- **Texture perm / afro perm** — chemical texture service; assess hair condition and previous chemical history first.
- **Extensions** — added material/hair; consider weight, attachment method, tension and removal plan.

Terminology can vary across communities. Confirm the intended structure with reference images and plain-language description before service.
