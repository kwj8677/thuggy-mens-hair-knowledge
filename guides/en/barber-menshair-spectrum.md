# Barbering and Men's-Hair Technique Spectrum

[한국어 원문](../barber-menshair-spectrum.md)

Real men's hair work is a combination of structure, tools, texture, finish and maintenance—not a strict split between “barber” and “salon.”

## Five useful dimensions
1. **Structure** — where weight and length remain or disappear.
2. **Tools** — clippers, scissors and razors are selected by the result required.
3. **Transition** — fade/taper, graduation and longer connections.
4. **Texture/finish** — sharp controlled surfaces vs softer movement.
5. **Maintenance** — what the client can realistically reproduce and maintain.

### Specialty/textured-hair work
Braids, cornrows, locs and afro/textured-hair services use different construction and maintenance methods. Consultation should confirm current texture, length, scalp/hair condition, previous chemical work, tension tolerance and intended wear time.
